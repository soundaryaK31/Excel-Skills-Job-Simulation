
# Excel Skills Job Simulation

## Overview

This repository contains my work from the **Excel Skills Job Simulation**.

The simulation involved working with account sales data to practice data cleaning, Excel formulas, conditional formatting, dashboard development, data visualization, and spreadsheet automation using macros and VBA.

## Tasks Completed

### Task 1 – Conditional Formatting
- Identified and highlighted formula errors.
- Highlighted missing values.
- Identified accounts not cross-sold with Product 2.
- Highlighted positive and negative 5-year CAGR values.
- Identified the top 10% of accounts by 2021 unit sales.

[View Conditional Formatting Workbook](workbooks/01_Conditional_Formatting.xlsx)

### Task 2 – Excel Formulas and Summary Calculations
- SUM – Total Unit Sales
- AVERAGE – Average Annual Unit Sales
- MAX – Highest Annual Unit Sales
- MIN – Lowest Annual Unit Sales
- IF – Sales Level classification
- IFERROR – Sales Change calculation
- COUNTIF – Count of accounts with Product 2
- SUMIF – Total 2021 unit sales for Retail accounts

[View Formula Practice Workbook](workbooks/02_Excel_Formulas_and_Summary.xlsx)

### Task 3 – Dashboard Development
Created a Dashboard worksheet containing:

1. Unit Sales by Year – Line Chart
2. Top 10 Accounts by 2021 Unit Sales – Bar Chart
3. 2021 Unit Sales by Account Type – Column Chart

[View Excel Dashboard](workbooks/03_Excel_Dashboard.xlsx)

### Task 4 – Macros and VBA Automation
Created macro-enabled Excel work to practice:

- Sorting the dataset by 5 YR CAGR in descending order.
- Sorting the dataset by 2021 Unit Sales in descending order.
- Using macros and VBA to automate repetitive sorting tasks.

[View Macro-enabled Workbook](workbooks/04_Excel_Macros_and_Automation.xlsm)
